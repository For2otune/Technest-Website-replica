Our story

TechNest was founded in 2021 by a small crew of self-confessed gadget nerds who were tired of choosing between "cheap and unreliable" and "expensive and overhyped." We wanted a third option.

So we started testing everything ourselves before it ever hit the shelf earbuds, smartwatches, speakers, and the odd weird gadget nobody asked for but everyone ends up loving. If it doesn't earn a spot in our own bags, it doesn't earn a spot in yours.

Today we ship to customers worldwide, but the standard hasn't changed: real testing, honest pricing, and gear that still works the way it should a year in.

What we stand for

Tested, not just listed

Customers over margins

We'd rather talk you out of a product you don't need than upsell you into one that doesn't fit.

Fast, honest support
